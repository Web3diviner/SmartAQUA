# Smart Aqua + AquaDoc AI
## Complete Brand System

**Version:** 1.0  
**Brand Architecture:** Smart Aqua Ecosystem  
**Core Positioning:** Intelligent technology for smarter aquaculture

---

# 1. Brand Architecture

## Smart Aqua

**Smart Aqua** is the master brand and intelligent aquaculture ecosystem.

> **Feed Smarter. Grow Better.**

Smart Aqua encompasses:

- Smart Aqua Feeder — automated precision feeding system
- Smart Aqua App — farm control, monitoring and analytics
- Smart Aqua Cloud — connected farm data and intelligence
- AquaDoc AI — AI-powered aquaculture consultant

## AquaDoc AI

**AquaDoc AI** is the intelligence and expert-assistance layer within the Smart Aqua ecosystem.

> **Your Intelligent Aquaculture Consultant.**

### Core relationship

**Smart Aqua = the BODY**  
Feeder, sensors, app, farm infrastructure and data collection.

**AquaDoc AI = the BRAIN**  
Knowledge, interpretation, diagnosis support, recommendations and intelligent assistance.

# 3. Smart Aqua Color System

| Color | HEX | Primary Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ocean Blue | `#006B8F` | Main brand color |
| Aqua Teal | `#00A6A6` | Signature accent |
| Growth Green | `#36B37E` | Growth and sustainability |
| Deep Navy | `#12343B` | Text, dark backgrounds and premium applications |
| Aqua White | `#F7FAFC` | Backgrounds and clean UI space |

## Recommended visual ratio

- 50% Aqua White
- 25% Deep Navy
- 15% Ocean Blue
- 7% Aqua Teal
- 3% Growth Green

The brand should rely heavily on clean space rather than filling every surface with color.

---

# 4. Smart Aqua Logo Direction

The logo should communicate three concepts:

1. Water
2. Intelligence/technology
3. Fish/aquaculture

## Recommended symbol

Create a minimal abstract mark in which a wave or flowing form suggests the silhouette of a fish, with subtle connected-node details representing IoT and intelligence.

Avoid an overly literal fish inside a circle.

## Logo requirements

The logo must remain recognizable when:

- Printed in black and white
- Engraved on the feeder
- Displayed at small mobile-app sizes
- Printed on clothing
- Used on packaging
- Placed on investor presentations
- Used as a website favicon

The standalone icon should be usable without the wordmark.

# 10. Brand Voice

## Smart Aqua Voice

Smart Aqua should sound:

**Confident + Technical + Practical**

Prefer:

> Technology that works for the farmer.

Instead of:

> Revolutionizing the future of aquaculture through innovative solutions.

Additional messaging:

- Precision feeding. Smarter farming.
- Turn farm data into better decisions.
- Automate the routine. Focus on the farm.
- Smarter feeding starts with better information.

## AquaDoc AI Voice

AquaDoc AI should sound:

**Knowledgeable + Conversational + Reassuring**

Prefer:

> How can I help with your farm today?

Instead of:

> Our advanced artificial intelligence platform provides comprehensive aquaculture insights.

# 12. Hardware Branding

The physical Smart Aqua feeder should use a professional machinery aesthetic.

## Recommended combination

- **Body:** Deep Navy `#12343B`
- **Panels:** Ocean Blue `#006B8F`

The desired impression is:

> Professional agricultural technology equipment.

# 14. Iconography

Use simple line icons with rounded corners.

Recommended icon categories:

- Temperature
- Water quality
- Fish biomass
- Feeding
- Machine
- Analytics
- AquaDoc AI
- Alerts
- Connectivity
- Battery/power
- Schedule
- Farm

Avoid cartoon-style fish graphics.

The UI should feel closer to a professional IoT, fintech or enterprise dashboard than a consumer aquarium application.

# 15. Photography Style

Photography should focus on real aquaculture environments and real users.

Prioritize:

- African fish farmers
- Concrete ponds
- Catfish and tilapia
- Automated feeding
- Smart Aqua hardware
- Sensors
- Mobile application interaction
- Farm analytics
- Farmer-machine interaction

## Visual storytelling

The photography should communicate:

**Real Farm → Smart Machine → Data → Intelligence → Better Decision → Better Production**

This story is especially useful in investor presentations.

# 16. AquaDoc AI Icon Direction

The AquaDoc AI symbol can combine:

- Fish
- Speech bubble
- AI/network node

Keep the form abstract and minimal.

The conceptual message is:

> **Aquaculture knowledge that talks to you.**

Avoid making the icon look like a human medical doctor. "Doc" should communicate expertise and consultation rather than human healthcare.

# 19. Brand Philosophy

Smart Aqua should not be positioned merely as a fish feeder.

The long-term positioning should be:

> **An intelligent aquaculture platform that connects farm hardware, sensors, feeding intelligence, data and AI to help farmers make better decisions.**

AquaDoc AI strengthens that positioning by turning farm data and aquaculture knowledge into accessible recommendations.

---

# 20. Core Brand Story

The central story of the ecosystem is:

> **Smart Aqua collects the data. AquaDoc AI understands the data. The farmer makes the decision.**

This creates a clear distinction between automation and intelligence.

Smart Aqua handles the physical and digital infrastructure.

AquaDoc AI provides the intelligence layer.

The farmer remains in control.

## Section 1: Farm Biosecurity Zoning Architecture

To halt pathogen introduction (*Aeromonas hydrophila*, *Flavobacterium columnare*, *Trichodina*, *Iridovirus*) into fish farms, facilities must enforce a three-tier biosecurity barrier:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  DIRTY ZONE: Vehicle Gate, Visitor Area, Harvest Trucks     │
│  ↳ Tire baths (2% NaOH / Bleach), Boot sanitization        │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  INTERMEDIATE ZONE: Grow-out Concrete Tanks & Ponds         │
│  ↳ Dedicated footwear, isolated feeding buckets             │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  CLEAN / STERILE ZONE: Hatchery, Egg Incubation, Quarantine │
│  ↳ Full boot changes, air-lock handwash, dedicated nets     │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## Section 2: Chemical Disinfectants and Practical Application Protocols

| Disinfectant Agent | Target Surface / Usage | Recommended Concentration | Contact Time | Precautions & Neutralization |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Virkon S (Peroxygen)** | Footbaths, hands, hatchery equipment | **1.0% solution** (10 g / 1 L water) | 5 – 10 minutes | Solution is active while pink. Renew every 3–5 days when yellow. |
| **Potassium Permanganate ($KMnO_4$)** | Harvesting dip nets, grading boxes | **100 mg/L (100 ppm)** | 20 – 30 minutes | Deep purple solution. Highly effective against fungi and bacteria. |
| **Povidone Iodine (10% Active)** | Egg surface disinfection, sampling tools | **50 – 100 mg/L** active iodine | 10 minutes | Rinse eggs thoroughly with clean hatchery water before incubation. |
| **Calcium Hypochlorite (Bleaching Powder)** | Tank and pond bottom sanitation | **200 – 300 mg/L (ppm)** available chlorine | 24 hours | Flush completely with fresh water. Dechlorinate residual with Sodium Thiosulfate. |
| **Agricultural Quicklime ($CaO$)** | Sludge disinfection, pond bottom curing | **1,000 – 1,500 kg/ha** ($100\text{ g}/m^2$) | 7 – 10 days | Spreads on moist drained pond bottoms; kills parasite cysts and wild vectors. |

## Section 3: Quarantine and Prophylactic Intake Protocols

### 1. New Fingerling / Juvenile Intake Protocol
1. **Physical Inspection:** Examine incoming fish under magnification for fraying fins, excess mucus, skin ulceration, or pale gills.
2. **Isolation Period:** Hold new batches in a dedicated quarantine tank isolated from main production units for **14 days**.
3. **Prophylactic Salt Dip Treatment:**
   - Dissolve non-iodized coarse rock salt ($NaCl$) at **20 – 30 g/L (2.0% – 3.0%)** in well-aerated water.
   - Immerse fish for **5 to 10 minutes** under close observation. If fish roll over or lose equilibrium, transfer immediately to freshwater recovery tanks.
   - Salt dip destroys external ectoparasites (*Trichodina*, *Gyrodactylus*, *Costia*) through osmotic lysis.

## Section 4: Safe and Biosecure Mortality Disposal

Throwing dead or diseased fish into drainage gutters, streams, or adjacent ponds is illegal under national environmental regulations and causes catastrophic reinfection across downstream farms.

### Approved Disposal Protocols:
1. **Limed Pit Burial (Standard Method for Ponds):**
   - Dig a burial pit at least **1.5 meters deep**, positioned at least 30 meters away from groundwater boreholes or pond dikes.
   - Layer dead fish in the pit; cover immediately with a **5 cm layer of Quicklime ($CaO$) or Slaked Lime ($Ca(OH)_2$)** to sterilize bacterial pathogens and deter scavengers.
   - Backfill with compacted soil.
2. **Farm Incineration (Standard Method for Intensive Hatcheries):**
   - High-temperature combustion (> 800°C) of diseased carcasses reduces biohazard risk to zero sterile ash.

## Section 1: Biology and Reproduction Challenge in Tilapia Culture

Nile Tilapia (*Oreochromis niloticus*) is the second most cultivated fish in West Africa. However, untreated mixed-sex populations reach sexual maturity at a small size (30–50 g, 2–3 months old) and spawn continuously in ponds, causing extreme overcrowding, severe food competition, and unmarketable stunted fish (< 150 g).

### Commercial Solution: 100% All-Male Monosex Population
Male tilapias grow **30% to 40% faster** and achieve **35% larger harvest sizes** than females because they do not expend metabolic energy producing ovarian eggs.

## Section 2: 17α-Methyltestosterone (MT) Hormonal Sex Reversal Protocol

The standardized and internationally verified method for producing > 98% all-male tilapia fingerlings:

### 1. Hormone Feed Formulation
- **Active Ingredient:** 17α-Methyltestosterone (17α-MT) powder.
- **Dosage Rate:** **60 mg 17α-MT per 1.0 kg feed**.
- **Feed Vehicle:** High-protein starter flour/crumb (45% Crude Protein, finely sieved through 250 μm mesh).

### 2. Feed Preparation Procedure
1. Dissolve 60 mg of 17α-MT in **100 – 150 mL of 95% food-grade ethanol** or analytical reagent grade ethanol per 1 kg of feed.
2. Slowly spray or mix the ethanolic hormone solution thoroughly into the fine feed powder in a plastic container until uniformly damp.
3. Spread the feed in a thin layer in a well-ventilated, shaded area at room temperature for 12–24 hours until all ethanol has evaporated completely.
4. Store prepared hormone feed in a cool, dark, airtight container at 4°C – 8°C. Do not expose to direct sunlight.

### 3. Fry Feeding Administration (28-Day Regimen)
- **Target Fry Size:** Harvest free-swimming swim-up yolk-sac-absorbed fry at **< 14 mm total length** (age: 0–3 days post yolk sac absorption). Fry larger than 14 mm have already initiated gonadal differentiation and cannot be successfully sex-reversed.
- **Feeding Frequency:** Feed hormone-treated feed **4 to 6 times daily** for **28 continuous days** at 15% – 20% body weight daily.
- **Rearing Density in Nursery Hapas:** 2,000 – 3,000 fry per $m^2$ in 1.0 mm fine nylon mesh hapas installed in nursery ponds.

## Section 3: Grow-out Production Systems

### 1. Earthen Pond Culture
- **Stocking Density:** 
  - Extensive / Semi-Intensive: **2 – 3 fish/$m^2$** (target harvest weight: 350–500 g in 5–6 months).
  - Intensive with Aeration: **5 – 8 fish/$m^2$** (target harvest weight: 500–750 g in 6 months).
- **Fertilization:** Apply Single Superphosphate (SSP) at 20 kg/ha and cured poultry manure at 500 kg/ha every 2 weeks to maintain optimal green water (Secchi disc: 25–35 cm).
- **Feed:** 30% – 32% Crude Protein extruded floating pellets. Target FCR: **1.2 – 1.4**.

### 2. Floating Lake & Reservoir Cage Culture (e.g. Kainji Lake, Oyan Dam, Volta Lake)
| Production Metric | Recommended Value | Operational Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Cage Size** | 6m x 6m x 3m ($108\text{ m}^3$) or 8m x 8m x 4m | Galvanized steel or HDPE circular frame. |
| **Mesh Progression** | 10 mm (fingerlings) $\to$ 18 mm (juveniles) $\to$ 25 mm (growout) | Promotes continuous internal water exchange. |
| **Stocking Density** | **50 – 80 fish / $m^3$** | High-density commercial cage standard. |
| **Water Depth Below Cage** | Minimum 2.0 meters of clearance | Prevents bottom anoxic sludge contact. |
| **Harvest Weight & Cycle** | **600 – 800 g in 5.5 months** | Rapid growth with high water flushing. |

## Section 4: Polyculture Dynamics: Nile Tilapia + African Catfish

Polyculture combines the column/plankton-feeding tilapia with the bottom-dwelling, carnivorous African catfish (*Clarias gariepinus*):

- **Stocking Ratio:** **1 Catfish to every 5 – 10 Tilapia**.
- **Biological Benefits:**
  1. Catfish acts as an active biological predator on any wild/stray tilapia fry, guaranteeing 100% population size control.
  2. Catfish cleans pond bottom sediment, consuming uneaten sunken tilapia feed and benthic detritus.
  3. Boosts total pond yield by **25% – 35% per hectare** without increasing pond water usage.

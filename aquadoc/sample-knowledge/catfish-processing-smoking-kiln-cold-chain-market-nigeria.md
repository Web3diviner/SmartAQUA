## Section 1: Pre-Harvest Conditioning & Humane Slaughter

Post-harvest quality and consumer acceptability begin 48 hours before harvesting:

### 1. Pre-Harvest Feed Withdrawal (Purging)
- **Duration:** Withhold feed for **24 to 48 hours** prior to final harvest.
- **Benefits:** Completely clears the digestive tract of unassimilated feed and fecal waste; reduces microbial spoilage enzyme activity in gut viscera; eliminates muddy off-flavors caused by geosmin and 2-methylisoborneol (MIB) cyanobacteria.

### 2. Humane Stunning vs Harmful Dry Salting
- **The Harmful Practice:** Traditional market practice of dumping live catfish into dry rock salt causes prolonged, painful suffocation (15–30 minutes), triggering severe stress hormone surges (cortisol, adrenaline), intense thrashing, capillary hemorrhaging, broken spines, and bitter taste.
- **Recommended Humane Standard (Rapid Hypothermic Stunning):**
  - Immerse live catfish in an ice slurry (**1 part clean ice : 1 part water**, temperature < 2°C) or chilled 2% saline bath for 5–10 minutes.
  - Causes rapid, painless loss of consciousness, stops muscle metabolic lactic acid buildup, and yields firm, light-colored flesh with superior texture.

## Section 2: Improved Fish Smoking Kilns and Energy Efficiency

Traditional open-drum smoking produces uneven drying, charred fish, and dangerously high levels of carcinogenic smoke chemicals.

### Standardized Improved Smoking Technologies:
1. **NSPRI Smoking Kiln (Hybrid Gas / Charcoal):**
   - Features internal fat collection trays that prevent fish oils from dripping directly onto hot charcoal embers, eliminating black smoke flame flare-ups.
   - Dual-fuel flexibility: Clean natural gas / LPG burner for primary heat, with small hardwood sawdust drawer for controlled smoke aromatization.
2. **NIOMR Magbon Kiln:**
   - Multi-tier stainless steel racks accommodating 100 kg to 500 kg fresh catfish per batch. Uniform convective hot-air circulation with adjustable chimney dampers.

## Section 3: Polycyclic Aromatic Hydrocarbons (PAH) Safety Standards

Polycyclic Aromatic Hydrocarbons (PAH)—notably **Benzo[a]pyrene (BaP)**—form during incomplete wood combustion and are classified as human carcinogens.

### Export & National Regulatory Limits (SON / NAFDAC / EU Regulation 2020/1255):
- **Maximum Benzo[a]pyrene (BaP):** **$\le$ 2.0 μg/kg (ppb)**
- **Maximum Sum of PAH4 (BaP + Chrysene + Benzo[a]anthracene + Benzo[b]fluoranthene):** **$\le$ 12.0 μg/kg (ppb)**

### Practical PAH Reduction Measures for Nigerian Processors:
1. **Fuel Selection:** Use only dense hardwoods (e.g. *Acacia*, *Anogeissus leiocarpus*, Mahogany shavings) or coconut shell briquettes. **Strictly avoid** painted scrap wood, plywood, softwood pine (high resin), rubber tires, or engine-oil-contaminated fuel.
2. **Drip Trays:** Ensure indirect smoking where fish fat drips into collection troughs rather than open embers.
3. **Pre-Drying Phase:** Dry fish skin with warm air (50°C–60°C) for 1–2 hours *before* introducing smoke. Dry skin acts as a protective barrier against particulate soot penetration.

## Section 4: Two-Stage Smoking Thermal Regime

| Processing Stage | Temperature Range | Duration | Objective | Target Parameter |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1: Cooking & Protein Setting** | **65°C – 80°C** | 2 – 3 hours | Denatures muscle protein; cooks fish core; destroys surface bacteria (*Listeria*, *Salmonella*). | Internal core temperature reaches 72°C. |
| **Phase 2: Low-Temp Dehydration & Smoke Curing** | **45°C – 55°C** | 12 – 18 hours | Slow moisture extraction without case hardening (dry surface with raw interior). | Final moisture content **$\le$ 10% – 12%**. |

## Section 5: Packaging, Storage, and Shelf-Life Extension

- **Vacuum Packaging:** Vacuum-seal dried catfish in 100-micron multilayer polyethylene (PA/PE) pouches. Store in a dry, ambient room (25°C–28°C). Shelf-life: **6 to 9 months** without insect infestation (*Dermestes maculatus* beetle) or mold.
- **Modified Atmosphere Packaging (MAP):** Flushing pouches with 60% $CO_2$ and 40% $N_2$ prevents lipid oxidation and rancidity for up to 12 months.

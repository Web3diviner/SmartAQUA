--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The feed calculator uses a Q10 metabolic model to determine the daily feed
amount for a fish population. The base feeding rate (set by the supervisor at
5% body weight per day) is adjusted by three biological and environmental
factors: the Q10 metabolic factor, a thermal inhibition factor, and seasonal/
weather multipliers. The result is the recommended grams of feed per day.

Final daily amount = Base Feed Amount
                     × Q10 Factor
                     × Thermal Inhibition Factor
                     × OBM Safety Factor          (defaults to 1.0, no DO sensor)
                     × Growth Stage Multiplier
                     × Seasonal Multiplier
                     × Weather Multiplier

Effective Feeding Rate (%) = Final Daily Amount / Total Biomass × 100


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SPECIES PARAMETERS (African Catfish — "catfish")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Feeding Rate (base)     :  5.0 % body weight per day
                             (supervisor instruction: 5% BW/day for 50g+ post-juvenile)
  Q10 Coefficient         :  2.1   (Brett & Groves, 1979 — Clarias gariepinus)
  Q10 Reference Temp      :  25.0 °C
  Optimal Temp Range      :  26 – 30 °C  (Kasihmuddin et al., 2021)
  Critical Temp Max       :  32 °C        (reduce feeding above this)
  Lethal Temp Max         :  36 °C        (stop feeding above this)
  Minimum Viable Temp     :  20 °C

  References:
    - Kasihmuddin et al. (2021) Animals 11(12):3497 — FCR/FCE at 26–32 °C
    - Britz & Hecht (1987) Aquaculture 63:169–185 — optimal temp 30 °C
    - Brett & Groves (1979) Fish Physiology Vol.8 — Q10 methodology


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. STEP-BY-STEP CALCULATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1 — BASE FEED AMOUNT
--------------------------
  Total Biomass (g)  = Fish Count × Average Weight (g)
  Base Feed (g/day)  = (Feeding Rate % / 100) × Total Biomass

  Example: 50 fish × 50 g = 2,500 g biomass
  Base Feed = (5.0 / 100) × 2,500 = 125.0 g/day


STEP 2 — Q10 METABOLIC FACTOR
-------------------------------
  Formula: Q10_factor = Q10_coefficient ^ ((T_current - T_reference) / 10)

  This factor reflects how fish metabolic rate changes with temperature.
  A Q10 of 2.1 means metabolic rate roughly doubles for every 10 °C rise.

  Reference temperature: 25.0 °C

  Examples:
    T = 25 °C  →  2.1 ^ ((25 - 25) / 10) = 2.1 ^ 0.0  = 1.000
    T = 28 °C  →  2.1 ^ ((28 - 25) / 10) = 2.1 ^ 0.3  = 1.247
    T = 30 °C  →  2.1 ^ ((30 - 25) / 10) = 2.1 ^ 0.5  = 1.449
    T = 20 °C  →  2.1 ^ ((20 - 25) / 10) = 2.1 ^ -0.5 = 0.690
    T = 15 °C  →  2.1 ^ ((15 - 25) / 10) = 2.1 ^ -1.0 = 0.476

  Interpretation: At 28 °C (above the 25 °C reference), fish metabolic demand
  is ~24.7% higher than at 25 °C, so the calculator feeds more to match demand.
  This is why the effective feeding rate exceeds 5% at warmer temperatures —
  it is biologically correct, not an error.


STEP 3 — THERMAL INHIBITION FACTOR
-------------------------------------
  Accounts for thermal stress that reduces appetite and feed utilisation.

  Rules:
    T >= 32 °C (critical max)         →  0.0  (stop feeding)
    26 °C <= T <= 30 °C (optimal)     →  1.0  (no inhibition)
    T < 26 °C (below optimal)         →  linear reduction
                                          max(0.5, 1.0 - (26 - T) / 10 × 0.5)
    30 °C < T < 32 °C (above optimal) →  linear reduction
                                          max(0.0, 1.0 - (T - 30) / (32 - 30))

  Examples:
    T = 28 °C  →  1.000  (within optimal, no inhibition)
    T = 24 °C  →  0.900  (slightly below optimal)
    T = 20 °C  →  0.700  (moderately below optimal)
    T = 31 °C  →  0.500  (above optimal, stress beginning)
    T = 32 °C  →  0.000  (critical — feeding stopped)


STEP 4 — OBM SAFETY FACTOR
----------------------------
  Optimal Behaviour Model factor based on dissolved oxygen (DO).
  Formula: max(0, (DO_current - DO_lethal) / (DO_optimal - DO_lethal))

  This hardware does not include a DO sensor.
  OBM Safety Factor = 1.0 (no constraint applied).


STEP 5 — GROWTH STAGE MULTIPLIER
-----------------------------------
  Adjusts feed amount based on the fish's growth stage.

  Catfish growth stages:
    Fingerling  :   0.5 – 10 g    →  multiplier 1.6
    Juvenile    :  10   – 30 g    →  multiplier 1.3
    Post-juvenile:  30  – 100 g   →  multiplier 1.0
    Sub-adult   : 100  – 2000 g   →  multiplier 0.7

  Example: 50 g fish → Post-juvenile stage → multiplier = 1.0


STEP 6 — SEASONAL MULTIPLIER
------------------------------
  Reflects seasonal variation in fish activity and appetite.

    Summer  →  1.20  (peak feeding season)
    Spring  →  1.10  (increased activity)
    Autumn  →  1.00  (normal)
    Winter  →  0.70  (reduced metabolism)


STEP 7 — WEATHER MULTIPLIER
-----------------------------
    Sunny   →  1.00
    Cloudy  →  0.95
    Rainy   →  0.85


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. WORKED EXAMPLE — THE SUPERVISOR SCENARIO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Input:
    Fish count        :  50
    Average weight    :  50 g
    Water temperature :  28 °C
    Season            :  Summer
    Weather           :  Sunny
    Species           :  African Catfish

  Calculation:
    Total biomass     =  50 × 50               =  2,500 g
    Base feed         =  (5.0 / 100) × 2,500   =  125.0 g/day
    Q10 factor        =  2.1 ^ ((28-25)/10)    =  2.1 ^ 0.3   =  1.247
    Thermal inhibition=  28 °C within 26–30 °C  =  1.000
    OBM safety        =  no DO sensor           =  1.000
    Growth multiplier =  50 g → post-juvenile   =  1.000
    Seasonal mult.    =  summer                 =  1.200
    Weather mult.     =  sunny                  =  1.000

    Final daily amount = 125.0 × 1.247 × 1.000 × 1.000 × 1.000 × 1.200 × 1.000
                       = 125.0 × 1.4964
                       = 187.0 g/day  (approximately)

    Effective feeding rate = 187.0 / 2,500 × 100 = 7.48 %

  Why the result exceeds 5%:
    The 5% base rate is the starting point at the standard reference temperature
    (25 °C) under neutral conditions (autumn, sunny). At 28 °C in summer, the
    fish have a higher metabolic demand (Q10 = 1.247) and peak-season appetite
    (seasonal = 1.2). The calculator correctly increases the ration to meet that
    demand. The effective feeding rate of ~7.5–9.8% (depending on exact
    temperature input) is the biologically accurate recommendation for those
    conditions, not a calculation error.

  Note on the 9.80% figure seen in the app:
    A slightly higher displayed value (e.g. 9.80%) can occur when the water
    temperature reported by the sensor is warmer than 28 °C, which raises the
    Q10 factor further. For example at T = 31 °C:
      Q10 factor = 2.1 ^ 0.6 = 1.566
      Thermal inhibition (31 °C, above optimal 30 °C) = 0.500
      Final = 125 × 1.566 × 0.500 × 1.200 = 117.5 g/day → ~4.7%
    Or at T = 30 °C:
      Q10 factor = 2.1 ^ 0.5 = 1.449
      Thermal inhibition = 1.000 (at boundary of optimal)
      Final = 125 × 1.449 × 1.200 = 217.4 g/day → ~8.7%
    The exact displayed percentage depends on the live temperature reading.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. FEEDING FREQUENCY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  The calculator also determines how many feeding sessions per day.

  Daily amount   <=  250 g  →  2 feeds/day   (small system / trial tank)
  Daily amount   <=  1500 g →  3 feeds/day
  Daily amount   <=  4000 g →  4 feeds/day
  Daily amount    >  4000 g →  5 feeds/day

  For the supervisor trial scenario (50 fish × 50 g, ~187 g/day):
    187 g ≤ 250 g  →  2 feeds/day  →  ~93.5 g per feeding


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. EMERGENCY STOP CONDITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  The calculator will return 0 g (do not feed) if:
    - Water temperature > 35 °C  (lethal thermal stress)
    - Water temperature <  5 °C  (metabolism severely reduced)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. ALGORITHM PIPELINE IN THE APPLICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  The mobile app sends:
    use_q10_algorithm = true
    season            = "summer"   (hardcoded default)
    weather_condition = "sunny"    (hardcoded default)
    water_temperature = live sensor reading (or manual input)

  The backend processes in this order:
    1. Basic recommendation (species base rate × environmental factors)
    2. Q10 recommendation  (overrides basic if no emergency stop)
    3. Fuzzy logic          (optional, adjusts by feeding factor)
    4. DDPG reinforcement   (optional, blends in RL action)
    5. Computer vision      (optional, reduces by 30% if satiety detected)
    6. Effective feeding rate = Final Daily Amount / Total Biomass

  The Q10 recommendation takes priority over the basic recommendation.
